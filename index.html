John Antony

Singer / Acoustic Guitarist, Popular Covers

Hi, I'm John Antony, a singer / acoustic guitarist based in Portland, Oregon. I perform feel-good cover songs spanning the 1960s to today, with a lively mix of pop, rock, and country favorites.

My performances focus on upbeat and easygoing songs people know and love - music that invites audiences to sing along, relax, and enjoy the moment.

I am available for bars, restaurants, private gatherings, and community events.
